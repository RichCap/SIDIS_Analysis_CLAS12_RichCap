The nominal results are obtained from the fully corrected $\phi_h$ distributions using the procedures described in \Cref{Analysis Procedure}. These include 3D Bayesian unfolding for the acceptance correction and a spline-weighted physics injection into the \textsc{clasdis} MC to improve its description of the underlying physics. This iterative method and its impact on the measurements will be discussed in \Cref{sys-mod-spline}.

The final measurements are shown as functions of $z$ in \Crefrange{fig:Moments_Spline_z_A_sys}{fig:Moments_Spline_z_C_sys} and as functions of $P_T$ in \Crefrange{fig:Moments_Spline_pT_A_sys}{fig:Moments_Spline_pT_C_sys}. The measurements and their individual fit and systematic uncertainties are listed in \Cref{tab:BinBordersText}. The methods used to determine the systematic uncertainties shown for each measurement will be discussed in \Cref{Systematic Uncertainties}.

The results will be compared with world data and theoretical models in \Cref{Conclusions}. These comparisons will place the measured moments in the context of previous measurements and theoretical expectations.
