kt2_results.tsv has one row for each attempted (Q2, y, z) extraction.
Blank numeric fields mean the quantity is undefined for that row.
Units: Q2 in GeV^2, P_T in GeV, P_T^2 and <k_T^2> in GeV^2. A0 is the fully corrected cross section after one P_T^2 normalization.

Slope rules:
A successful fit always stores inv_kt2 = -a and inv_kt2_err = sigma_a.
near_zero_slope: |a| < 1e-12, or a is not finite. kt2 and kt2_err are then blank.
slope_consistent_with_zero: |a| < 2 * sigma_a. kt2 = -1/a and kt2_err = sigma_a / a^2 are still stored when the slope is numerically invertible.
positive_slope: a > 0. The slope is not forced through zero, and kt2 is negative.
poor: chi2/ndf > 2.0. log_uncertainty_large: at least one used point has sigma_A/A > 0.5.
cov_ab is the slope-intercept covariance Cov(b, a) = Cov(parameter 0, parameter 1).
ln_c0_err = b_err. c0 = exp(b). c0_err = exp(b) * b_err.
kt2_err = sigma_a / a^2 when kt2 is defined.

pT2 is the square of the P_T bin midpoint, not the midpoint of the squared borders.
Normalization is applied once: A0 = Fit_Par_A / (bin area * luminosity * photon flux), with Delta(pT2) in the bin area.
